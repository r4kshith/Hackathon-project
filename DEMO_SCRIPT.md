# 3-minute demo

## 0:00–0:30 — Start a store

Open `/signup`, create an owner account, and start the four-step setup. Enter a store name and contact details, upload a logo, select two categories, then open the theme previews and compare their layout, typography, and color together.

## 0:30–1:00 — Populate and publish

Choose **Create my store**. The selected categories fill with sample products and route to `/s/{slug}`. Point out the hero, category cards, search, and price/in-stock filters. Open a product and add it to the cart.

## 1:00–1:30 — Place an order

Continue to checkout, enter shipping details, and place the order using the clearly labeled cash-on-delivery demo option. Show the confirmation and order number.

## 1:30–2:10 — Manage the shop

Open `/admin`. Show the revenue/order overview, weekly chart, store health suggestions, and low-stock watch. Open **Products & stock** to adjust inventory or try the CSV/XLSX import preview. Open **Orders** and advance the demo order to **Packed**; point out the visible test notification log (no email is sent).

## 2:10–3:00 — Ask the assistant

In **Store assistant**, ask “What are my top 5 selling products this month?” and expand/show the returned data table. Ask “Which products are low on stock?” Then try “Delete all products” or “Show me Store B's revenue”; the assistant refuses because it is read-only and scoped to this store. For repeatable seeded data, sign in with `owner@demo.launch` / `launch-demo-2026`.
